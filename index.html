TUSHM Autopost

TUSHM Autopost is an internal publishing tool used by the TUSHM team to schedule and publish our own promotional videos and animated episodes to our own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Every video, cover and caption is reviewed and approved by a TUSHM team member before it is scheduled. The tool does not access or publish content for any other users.
