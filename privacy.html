Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

TUSHM Autopost ("the App") is an internal tool operated by the TUSHM team to publish our own content to our own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok / Instagram account (account ID, username, display name, avatar) so we can show which account a post will go to.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Meta, used only to upload and publish videos that our team has approved.

How we use it

Data is used solely to publish TUSHM's own videos, covers and captions at scheduled times, and to show the publishing status inside the App. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens and post records are stored locally on a TUSHM-controlled computer and are never sent to third parties other than TikTok and Meta themselves.

Retention and deletion

You may revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok or Instagram settings. When access is revoked or upon request, we delete the stored tokens and account information within 7 days. To request deletion, email CONTACT_EMAIL.
